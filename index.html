Globus is a non-profit service developed and operated by the University of Chicago.

Globus thanks the US Department of Energy, the National Science Foundation, the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, the National Institutes of Health, and above all its more than 250 subscribers worldwide for the support that has allowed us to develop and operate the service.

A note on coverage. The transfer logs record 59.4 million transfers moving 131 billion files and 3.6 exabytes. IP addresses are not recorded for Globus Connect Personal endpoints — laptops and personal machines — so only transfers with an IP recorded at both ends can be placed on a map: 34 million transfers (57%), carrying 70 billion files (54%) and 2.3 exabytes (63%) of the total. Per-country figures are therefore lower bounds, and chiefly reflect traffic between institutional (Globus Connect Server) endpoints.

Data: Globus transfer logs, July 2010 – November 2025. Distances are great-circle; locations are IP-level geolocation.
